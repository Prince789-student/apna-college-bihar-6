38 Government Engineering Colleges in Bihar

Verified institutional profiles, UGEAC closing cutoffs, seat matrices, campus facilities, and placement statistics for all 38 government colleges under Bihar Engineering University.

Evolution of Technical Education in Bihar: The 38-District Network

Higher technical education in Bihar has witnessed a historic transformation over the last decade. Historically anchored by legendary institutions like Muzaffarpur Institute of Technology (MIT Muzaffarpur, Estd. 1954) and Bhagalpur College of Engineering (BCE Bhagalpur, Estd. 1960), the state government launched an ambitious initiative under the "Saat Nishchay" good governance program to establish a dedicated, modern Government Engineering College (GEC) in every single one of Bihar's 38 administrative districts.

To unify academic standards, syllabus frameworks, and examination timelines, the Government of Bihar enacted the Bihar Engineering University Act, 2021, officially creating the Bihar Engineering University (BEU Patna). Today, all 38 government engineering colleges function under the single academic umbrella of BEU, ensuring AICTE-compliant curriculum standards, standardized semester evaluations, and transparent degree awards.

Admission Workflow via NTA JEE Main & BCECEB UGEAC

Admissions across all 38 state engineering colleges are 100% merit-based and centralized. The Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) conducts the annual Under Graduate Engineering Admission Counselling (UGEAC) using National Testing Agency (NTA) JEE Main All India Ranks. The admission sequence involves:

  1. Online Registration: Candidates with valid JEE Main scores register on bceceboard.bihar.gov.in and receive a State UGEAC Merit Rank.
  2. Choice Filling & Locking: Candidates select preferred college-branch combinations from the 38 government colleges based on closing cutoff trends.
  3. Seat Allotment (Rounds 1, 2, and Mop-Up): Computerized allocation based on category reservation (UR, EWS, BC, EBC, SC, ST, RCG, DQ) and candidate merit.
  4. Physical Document Verification: Allotted candidates report to designated reporting nodal centers with original academic and domicile credentials.

Subsidized Government Fee Structure

Because these institutions are state-funded, annual academic tuition fees are heavily subsidized:

  • Tuition Fee: Subsidized to approximately ₹120 per year (₹10/month).
  • Annual Academic & Development Fee: Between ₹10,000 and ₹14,000 per academic year.
  • Hostel & Messing: Hostel room rent is nominal (~₹2,400/year), with student-managed cooperative mess charges averaging ₹3,000 to ₹3,500 per month.
  • Bihar Student Credit Card (MNSSBY): Eligible students can avail zero-interest education loans up to ₹4,00,000 for academic and living expenses.
