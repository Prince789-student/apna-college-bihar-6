BEU Attendance Tracker & 75% Rule Guide

Understand Bihar Engineering University's mandatory 75% attendance ordinance. Calculate your branch attendance, estimate classes required to clear exam eligibility, and learn medical condonation procedures.

The BEU 75% Mandatory Attendance Regulation

As codified in the academic ordinances of Bihar Engineering University (BEU Patna), every registered undergraduate engineering candidate is strictly required to attend a minimum of 75% of the total theory lectures, tutorials, and practical laboratory sessions held in each course during the semester.

General Threshold

75% Standard

Full eligibility for university end-semester examination admit cards without penalty or special permissions.

Medical Condonation

65% to 74%

Permitted only upon submission of authorized medical certificates signed by competent medical officers and approved by the Principal.

Debarment Risk

Below 65%

Strict debarment from university examinations. Candidate is awarded an 'F' grade and must re-register or take carry-over exams.

How to Calculate Required Classes to Reach 75%

If your current attendance percentage has fallen below 75%, use the following standard formula to calculate the exact number of consecutive upcoming classes you must attend:

Required Classes = ⌈(3 × Total Classes Held - 4 × Classes Attended)⌉

Example: If 60 classes have been held and you attended 35 → (3 × 60 - 4 × 35) = 180 - 140 = 40 more classes must be attended consecutively.

Medical Condonation Procedure

If genuine illness or hospitalization caused an attendance shortfall below 75%:

  1. Submit a formal medical leave application to your Head of Department (HOD) within 7 days of returning to campus.
  2. Attach prescription slips, discharge summary, and fitness certificate issued by a registered government medical officer.
  3. The college academic council reviews applications and may grant a relaxation of up to 10% (bringing the effective threshold down to 65%).
