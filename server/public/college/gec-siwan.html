About Government Engineering College, Siwan

Government Engineering College, Siwan (GEC Siwan) is located in the land of India's first President Dr. Rajendra Prasad at Pachrukhi, Siwan. Spread over 20 acres, it features top-notch smart classrooms, automated library, modern laboratories, and vibrant student coding culture.

Operating under the academic guidelines of Bihar Engineering University (BEU Patna) and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), GEC Siwan offers four-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree programs across core engineering branches. The curriculum follows the standard AICTE Choice Based Credit System (CBCS), integrating theoretical rigor, laboratory experimentation, and industrial internships.

Admission Process & Required Verification Documents

Admissions to B.Tech programs at GEC Siwan are conducted strictly through BCECEB UGEAC based on NTA JEE Main scores. Candidates must qualify 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry with at least 45% marks (40% for reserved categories).

Mandatory Documents Required for Physical Reporting:

  • ✓ JEE Main Rank Card & Admit Card
  • ✓ BCECEB UGEAC Allotment Letter
  • ✓ 10th & 12th Marksheet & Passing Certificate
  • ✓ Residential / Domicile Certificate of Bihar
  • ✓ Caste Certificate (if claiming reservation)
  • ✓ Income Certificate (EWS / TFW candidates)
  • ✓ Medical Fitness Certificate
  • ✓ Character Certificate & Migration Certificate
  • ✓ 6 Passport Size Photographs
