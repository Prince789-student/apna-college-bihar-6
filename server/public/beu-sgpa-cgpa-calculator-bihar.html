BEU SGPA Formula — Manual Calculation

SGPA Formula:
SGPA = Σ (Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ (Total Credits)

Example:
Subject 1: Math — 4 credits, Grade A (6.0 GP) = 24
Subject 2: Physics — 3 credits, Grade B+ (5.5 GP) = 16.5
Subject 3: Chemistry — 3 credits, Grade O (7.0 GP) = 21
Total Credits = 10, Total Points = 61.5
SGPA = 61.5 ÷ 10 = 6.15
SGPA to Percentage Conversion (BEU Official Formula):
Percentage = (SGPA − 0.75) × 10

Example: SGPA 6.15 → (6.15 − 0.75) × 10 = 54%

Frequently Asked Questions — BEU SGPA Calculator

Q: BEU SGPA kaise calculate hota hai?
BEU SGPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ(Total Credits). BEU 7-point grading scale use karta hai. Apna College Bihar ke free calculator mein grades enter karo — automatic result milega.
Q: BEU SGPA to percentage kaise convert karein?
BEU official formula: Percentage = (SGPA − 0.75) × 10. SGPA 6.5 hai to Percentage = (6.5 − 0.75) × 10 = 57.5%. Calculator automatically yeh bhi dikhata hai.
Q: BEU mein CGPA aur SGPA mein kya fark hai?
SGPA sirf ek semester ka average hai. CGPA sabhi semesters ka weighted average hai. BEU final degree mein CGPA print hota hai, isliye CGPA zyada important hai career mein.
Q: BEU mein first division ke liye CGPA kitna chahiye?
BEU mein First Division ke liye CGPA 5.5 ya usse zyada hona chahiye (equivalent to ~60% marks). CGPA 6.0+ = First Division with Distinction. CGPA 4.5–5.4 = Second Division.
