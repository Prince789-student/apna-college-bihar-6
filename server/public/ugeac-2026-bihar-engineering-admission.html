UGEAC 2026 Important Highlights

The Bihar Engineering Admission process (Under Graduate Engineering Admission Counselling — UGEAC) is the official, centralized counselling mechanism conducted by BCECEB for students who have appeared in NTA JEE Main and seek admission into first-year B.Tech degree programs across 38 Government Engineering Colleges (GECs) and participating state universities under Bihar Engineering University (BEU Patna).

  • Organizing Authority: Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB), IAS Association Building, Near Patna Airport, Patna.
  • Qualifying Examination: NTA JEE (Main) 2026 Paper-1 (B.E. / B.Tech).
  • Participating Institutes: 38 Government Engineering Colleges, MIT Muzaffarpur, BCE Bhagalpur, and university departments.
  • Total Seats Available: Over 10,800+ B.Tech engineering seats across traditional and modern emerging specializations.
  • Selection Criterion: Merit ranking formulated strictly according to National Testing Agency (NTA) JEE Main percentile scores.

Step-by-Step UGEAC 2026 Admission Procedure

Every prospective engineering scholar must adhere to the structured timeline outlined by BCECEB:

  1. Online Registration: Candidates initiate registration using their JEE Main Application Number and date of birth, followed by email and SMS OTP authentication.
  2. Application Form & Fee Submission: Filling personal, educational, category (General, EWS, BC, EBC, SC, ST, DQ) details and remitting the non-refundable counselling fee (₹1,200 for General/BC/EBC; ₹600 for SC/ST/DQ).
  3. Rank Card Publication: BCECEB releases state-specific UGEAC Merit Ranks (General Merit Rank and Category Ranks) determining admission precedence.
  4. Online Choice Filling & Locking: Aspirants systematically select branch and college preferences. ACB recommends filling at least 40+ choices in descending priority order.
  5. Provisional Seat Allotment: Allotment orders published across Round 1 and Round 2 with options to Freeze or Auto-Upgrade (Float).
  6. In-Person Document Verification: Mandatorily reporting to the designated verification nodal centre with authentic certificates, check slips, and biometrics.

Reservation Matrix & Category Guidelines

Bihar State reservation norms are strictly observed during seat allocation:

  • Extremely Backward Class (EBC): 25% quota allocated with state non-creamy layer certificate requirement.
  • Backward Class (BC): 18% quota for eligible BC candidates.
  • Scheduled Caste (SC): 20% dedicated reservation.
  • Scheduled Tribe (ST): 2% dedicated reservation.
  • Economically Weaker Section (EWS): 10% horizontal allocation for eligible general category aspirants.
  • 33% Horizontal Reservation for Girls: Bihar Government reserves one-third of total engineering seats exclusively for female domicile candidates, vastly increasing admission chances.
