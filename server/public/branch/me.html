B.Tech Specialization

Mechanical Engineering (ME)

Thermal Systems, Machine Design, Fluid Power & Advanced Manufacturing

Mechanical Engineering forms the industrial backbone of engineering technology. The BEU curriculum thoroughly trains students in thermodynamics, refrigeration, fluid machinery, machine element design, CAD/CAM, and automated industrial manufacturing.

Official 8-Semester Syllabus Breakdown

BEU Patna Regulation

Semester 1 & 2 (1st Year) BEU Curriculum

  • •Engineering Mechanics
  • •Basic Electrical Engineering
  • •Mathematics I & II
  • •Physics for Engineers
  • •Workshop Practice & Manufacturing Processes

Semester 3 (2nd Year) BEU Curriculum

  • •Thermodynamics
  • •Strength of Materials (Solid Mechanics)
  • •Material Science & Metallurgy
  • •Machine Drawing & CAD
  • •Mathematics III

Semester 4 (2nd Year) BEU Curriculum

  • •Applied Thermodynamics (IC Engines & Gas Turbines)
  • •Fluid Mechanics & Machinery
  • •Kinematics of Machines
  • •Manufacturing Processes I (Casting, Forming & Welding)
  • •Measurement & Instrumentation

Semester 5 (3rd Year) BEU Curriculum

  • •Heat and Mass Transfer (HMT)
  • •Dynamics of Machines
  • •Design of Machine Elements I
  • •Manufacturing Processes II (Machining & Machine Tools)
  • •Industrial Engineering & Operations Research

Semester 6 (3rd Year) BEU Curriculum

  • •Refrigeration & Air Conditioning (RAC)
  • •Design of Machine Elements II
  • •Computer Aided Design & Manufacturing (CAD/CAM)
  • •Internal Combustion Engines
  • •Automobile Engineering

Semester 7 & 8 (4th Year) BEU Curriculum

  • •Power Plant Engineering
  • •Mechatronics & Industrial Robotics
  • •Finite Element Methods (FEM)
  • •Major Industrial Capstone Project
  • •Industrial Training & Viva
