How to get an Internship in 2nd Year of B.Tech

📅 Published: 28 June 2026 ✍️ Written by: Er. Prince Kumar (BEU Mentor & ACB Team) ⏱️ 5 min read

Why Starting Your Internship Hunt in 2nd Year is a Game-Changer

Most engineering undergraduates make the critical mistake of postponing their internship search until their pre-final (6th semester) or final year. By that time, hundreds of students from your college and thousands across Bihar are competing for the exact same campus placement roles.

Securing an internship in your 2nd year (3rd or 4th semester) creates an extraordinary competitive advantage:

  • Pre-Placement Offer (PPO) Potential: Startups and tech companies frequently extend full-time placement offers to interns who perform well.
  • Resume Differentiation: While your peers show only academic course projects, your resume showcases production software, team collaboration, and real-world metrics.
  • Compliance with AICTE Internship Policy: The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) mandates minimum internship credits (between 14 to 20 contact credits across 4 years) for award of degree.

This comprehensive guide details the practical steps, portfolio blueprints, and cold-emailing strategies to land your first tech internship as a 2nd-year student.

---

Step 1: Master ONE Marketable Skill Track

You cannot land an internship by merely knowing basic C programming syntax. Companies hire interns to solve specific, immediate engineering bottlenecks. Choose one of the following four high-demand tracks and master it over 3 to 4 months:

Track A: Full-Stack Web Development (Highest Hiring Volume)

  • Frontend: Modern JavaScript (ES6+), React.js, Tailwind CSS.
  • Backend: Node.js with Express, or Python with FastAPI.
  • Database: PostgreSQL or MongoDB with Prisma/Mongoose ORM.
  • Key Deliverable: Build a functional SaaS web application with user authentication, database CRUD operations, and payment gateway or API integration.

Track B: Mobile Application Development

  • Framework: Flutter (Dart) or React Native.
  • Key Deliverable: Publish at least one clean mobile utility application to the Google Play Store showcasing responsive UI, state management (Bloc/Provider/Redux), and REST API consumption.

Track C: Data Analytics & Python Automation

  • Core Skills: Python, Pandas, NumPy, SQL, Power BI, Web Scraping (BeautifulSoup/Playwright).
  • Key Deliverable: Create end-to-end data pipeline dashboards analyzing public datasets (e.g., Bihar agricultural metrics or financial markets) with interactive visualizations.

Track D: IoT & Embedded Systems (For Core Branches: EE/ECE/Mech)

  • Core Skills: Embedded C, ESP32 / STM32 programming, MQTT protocol, sensor interfacing.
  • Key Deliverable: Build a functioning hardware prototype (e.g., remote agricultural sensor node or energy monitor) documented with circuit schematics and GitHub code.

---

Step 2: Build a Professional GitHub & LinkedIn Presence

Your online presence is your modern engineering resume. Before sending a single application:

  1. GitHub Portfolio:
  • Pin your top 3 completed projects.
  • Write an immaculate README.md for every repository: Include an architecture overview, screenshots or a live demo GIF, installation instructions, and tech stack badges.
  • Host live working demos using Vercel, Netlify, or Render. An interviewer is 10x more likely to click a working link than clone your repository.
  1. LinkedIn Optimization:
  • Headline: Avoid generic "Student at GEC X". Instead, write: "Full Stack Developer | React, Node.js, PostgreSQL | B.Tech CSE '28".
  • Featured Section: Link your GitHub profile, your live portfolio website, and key project demo links.
  • Content: Post brief, authentic updates documenting what you build, challenges you debug, and technologies you learn.

---

Step 3: Where to Find 2nd-Year Internships

Do not waste time applying to multinational giants like Google or Microsoft in your 2nd year; their formal campus summer programs are strictly restricted to 3rd-year students. Instead, focus on high-yield avenues:

  • Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent): The premier platform for early-stage and seed-funded startups. Founders and engineering leads personally review incoming applications.
  • Internshala & Unstop: Filter specifically for "Work from Home" or "Part-Time" internships during semester months, and full-time internships during summer breaks.
  • AICTE Internship Portal: The government portal (internship.aicte-india.org) frequently posts verified internships with government departments, NHAI, smart city projects, and technical organizations.
  • Open Source Fellowship Programs: Apply to global open-source programs like Google Summer of Code (GSoC), LFX Mentorship, and Hacktoberfest. Open source contributions carry more credibility on a resume than standard corporate internships.

---

Step 4: The Cold Email Blueprint

Cold emailing startup founders, CTOs, and Engineering Managers on LinkedIn or Twitter/X is the single highest-converting outreach channel for 2nd-year engineers.

Tested Cold Email Template:

Subject: Full-Stack Intern Application | [Your Name] | Built [Project Name]

>

Dear [Founder / Engineering Lead Name],

>

I hope this email finds you well. I follow [Company Name]'s recent work on [specific product feature or mission], and I was thoroughly impressed by how you solved [specific problem].

>

I am a 2nd-year B.Tech student at Bihar Engineering University specializing in [Your Branch]. Over the past 6 months, I have built production-level web applications using React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL.

>

Recently, I engineered [Project Name] ([Live Demo Link] | [GitHub Link]), a platform that [1-sentence description of what problem it solves and metrics achieved].

>

I would love the opportunity to contribute to [Company Name]'s engineering team as an intern for 2–3 months. I am eager to help your team with [specific tasks, e.g., building frontend components, writing API tests, or handling bug fixes].

>

My resume and portfolio are attached here: [Portfolio Link].

>

Thank you for your time and consideration.

>

Warm regards,
[Your Name]
B.Tech Candidate, [Your College Name]
GitHub: [Link] | LinkedIn: [Link] | Phone: [Number]

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Can I get an official No Objection Certificate (NOC) from my college in 2nd year?

Most BEU colleges readily issue an NOC for summer or winter vacation internships (June–July or December–January). For internships during the academic semester, verify with your Head of Department (HOD) whether the company permits flexible remote work outside lecture hours.

Q2: Should I accept an unpaid internship?

For your very first 2nd-year internship, prioritize learning, mentorship from senior engineers, and receiving an official Certificate of Completion and LinkedIn recommendation. However, never pay any agency for an internship—legitimate internships never charge students fees.
