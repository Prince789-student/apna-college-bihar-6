Top 10 Government Engineering Colleges in Bihar under UGEAC

📅 Published: 15 May 2026 ✍️ Written by: Er. Prince Kumar (BEU Mentor & ACB Team) ⏱️ 5 min read

Overview of UGEAC and Technical Education in Bihar

Over the last decade, technical education in Bihar has undergone a monumental transformation. With the formal establishment of the Bihar Engineering University (BEU) and the unified counselling framework administered by the Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB), admissions into state engineering colleges have become transparent, merit-driven, and highly competitive.

Every academic year, thousands of engineering aspirants participate in UGEAC (Undergraduate Engineering Admission Counselling). Selection is determined exclusively by the National Testing Agency's (NTA) JEE Main rank. With 38 government engineering colleges across the state, deciding how to arrange your preference list during choice filling can determine the trajectory of your professional career.

This in-depth guide evaluates the top 10 government engineering colleges in Bihar, assessing their legacy, placement metrics, laboratory infrastructure, faculty strength, and previous year cutoff trends to help you make an informed decision.

---

The Top 10 Government Engineering Colleges Ranked

1. Muzaffarpur Institute of Technology (MIT), Muzaffarpur

  • Established: 1954
  • Campus Area: 55+ Acres
  • Key Branches: Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Information Technology, Electronics & Communication.
  • Academic Reputation: MIT Muzaffarpur is widely regarded as the premier engineering institute in Bihar. Established shortly after independence, its legacy is backed by a prestigious alumni network spanning global tech leaders, senior IAS/IES officers, and PSU executives.
  • Placements & Packages: MIT consistently leads state placements. IT giants like TCS, Wipro, and Cognizant recruit in large numbers, while core infrastructure firms like L&T, Prism Johnson, and Shapoorji Pallonji hire civil and mechanical graduates. Average packages range between ₹4.2 LPA and ₹6.5 LPA, with highest off-campus offers exceeding ₹18 LPA.
  • Campus Life: Well-equipped workshops, Central Library with 50,000+ technical volumes, dedicated sports grounds, active IEEE student branch, and reliable on-campus hostels.

2. Bhagalpur College of Engineering (BCE), Bhagalpur

  • Established: 1960
  • Campus Area: 250+ Acres (One of the largest in Eastern India)
  • Key Branches: Computer Science & Engineering, Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics.
  • Academic Reputation: Situated along the banks of the Ganges, BCE Bhagalpur is renowned for its academic rigor. It has produced prominent engineers, including notable space scientists and researchers.
  • Placements & GATE Culture: BCE has an exceptional GATE qualification culture. A significant percentage of final-year students qualify for GATE and secure M.Tech admissions at premier IITs and NITs or join PSUs like IOCL, NTPC, and PowerGrid. Campus recruitment is strong for CSE and Civil disciplines.

3. Gaya College of Engineering (GCE), Gaya

  • Established: 1980 (Revamped 2008)
  • Key Branches: Computer Science & Engineering, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical & Electronics.
  • Academic Reputation: GCE Gaya has emerged as one of the fastest-growing institutes under BEU. Supported by TEQIP-III funding, the college features modern computer laboratories, high-speed campus networking, and smart classrooms.
  • Placements: Cisco, Tech Mahindra, and numerous mid-sized software startups participate in campus hiring. Dedicated placement training sessions are organized from the 6th semester onward.

4. Darbhanga College of Engineering (DCE), Darbhanga

  • Established: 1999 (Formerly Jagannath Mishra Institute of Technology)
  • Key Branches: Computer Science, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical & Electronics.
  • Academic Reputation: Renowned for disciplined academic schedules and punctual semester execution. DCE is favored by students seeking a balanced academic curriculum with strong laboratory exposure in structural and thermal engineering.

5. Motihari College of Engineering (MCE), Motihari

  • Established: 1986
  • Key Branches: Computer Science, Civil, Mechanical, Electrical.
  • Infrastructure Highlights: MCE has received significant state grants for new departmental academic blocks, advanced computing centers, and boys/girls hostel facilities. The active coding society conducts regular hackathons.

6. Nalanda College of Engineering (NCE), Chandi

  • Established: 2008
  • Key Branches: Computer Science, Aeronautical, Civil, Mechanical, Electrical.
  • Unique Advantage: Located near the historic knowledge capital of Nalanda, NCE offers a peaceful academic environment. It is one of the few government colleges in Bihar offering an undergraduate degree in Aeronautical Engineering.

7. Loknayak Jai Prakash Institute of Technology (LNJPIT), Chapra

  • Established: 2012
  • Key Branches: Computer Science, Civil, Mechanical, Electrical & Electronics.
  • Campus Culture: LNJPIT is distinguished by its student-driven developer clubs. The competitive coding community actively participates in Smart India Hackathon (SIH), CodeChef contests, and open-source development.

8. Bakhtiyarpur College of Engineering (BCE), Patna

  • Established: 2016
  • Location Advantage: Proximity to Patna gives BCE Bakhtiyarpur a major edge. Students frequently attend technical seminars, hackathons, and industrial exhibitions in the state capital. Guest lectures by IIT Patna professors and IT executives are regularly hosted.

9. Purnea College of Engineering (PCE), Purnea

  • Established: 2017
  • Key Branches: Computer Science, Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, ECE.
  • Development: Serving students from Seemanchal and North-East Bihar, PCE features modern lab apparatus, dedicated language labs, and a proactive Training and Placement Cell.

10. Saharsa College of Engineering (SCE), Saharsa

  • Established: 2017
  • Key Branches: Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics & Communication.
  • Growth: A rapidly progressing institute with dedicated faculty members recruited through the Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC). Strong academic focus on core engineering fundamentals.

4-Step Strategic Choice Filling Guideline for UGEAC

  1. Prioritize Branch Over Location for Tech Careers: If your primary objective is a career in software development, cloud computing, or artificial intelligence, prioritize CSE and IT across the top 6-8 colleges before settling for non-preferred core branches in top-tier colleges.
  2. Cluster Top Legacy Colleges First: Always place MIT Muzaffarpur and BCE Bhagalpur at the top of your choice list for all acceptable branches due to their established alumni networks and corporate recruiter relationships.
  3. Account for Living and Commuting Logistics: For newer colleges, verify whether on-campus hostel allotments are guaranteed for 1st-year students or if private accommodations nearby are accessible and safe.
  4. Utilize the Apna College Bihar Predictor: Before locking choices, cross-reference your exact JEE Main score with the ACB UGEAC Predictor to construct a balanced list containing Dream, Realistic, and Safe college options.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Are hostel facilities available for first-year students in all government engineering colleges in Bihar?

Yes, all 38 government engineering colleges have designated boys and girls hostels on their permanent campuses. However, in high-density colleges, 1st-year allotments are distributed based on merit and distance from home.

Q2: Can I get admission through UGEAC without appearing for JEE Main?

No. Under current BCECEB regulations, admission to government B.Tech programs in Bihar requires a valid NTA JEE Main scorecard. BCECEB does not conduct a separate entrance test for engineering admissions.

Q3: What is the total tuition fee for 4-year B.Tech in Bihar government engineering colleges?

Tuition fees across all BEU-affiliated government institutions are nominal, typically around ₹10,000 to ₹12,000 per year, making technical education in Bihar one of the most cost-effective in India.

Q4: Does the Bihar Student Credit Card (BSCC) cover all expenses in these colleges?

Yes. The Bihar Student Credit Card scheme provides education loans of up to ₹4 Lakhs at subsidized interest rates, covering tuition, hostel charges, mess fees, laptops, and academic books.
