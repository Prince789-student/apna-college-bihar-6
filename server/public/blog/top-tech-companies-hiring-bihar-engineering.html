Top Tech Companies Hiring in Bihar Engineering Colleges

📅 Published: 28 June 2026 ✍️ Written by: Er. Prince Kumar (BEU Mentor & ACB Team) ⏱️ 5 min read

The Evolving Campus Placement Landscape in Bihar

In recent years, the placement ecosystem across Bihar's government engineering colleges has undergone significant expansion. Through centralized pool campus drives coordinated under the Department of Science, Technology and Technical Education, as well as institutional Training & Placement (T&P) cells, major multinational corporations and domestic infrastructure leaders actively recruit graduates from MIT Muzaffarpur, BCE Bhagalpur, GCE Gaya, DCE Darbhanga, and BCE Bakhtiyarpur.

For students enrolled in Bihar Engineering University (BEU), understanding who visits your campus, what technical proficiencies they expect, and how compensation packages are tiered is essential to preparing an effective career roadmap.

This comprehensive guide breaks down the major IT and core engineering recruiters, salary structures, selection rounds, and preparation strategies.

---

Top IT Recruiters & Compensation Tiers

The information technology sector represents the largest volume of campus placements for CSE, IT, ECE, and core branch students with coding proficiency.

1. Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)

TCS conducts nationwide campus hiring primarily through the TCS NQT (National Qualifier Test), offering three distinct entry-level profiles:

  • TCS Ninja: ₹3.36 LPA to ₹3.6 LPA. General software development, support, and systems engineering roles.
  • TCS Digital: ₹7.0 LPA to ₹7.5 LPA. Advanced software engineering roles involving cloud architectures, data analytics, and modern web frameworks.
  • TCS Prime: ₹9.0 LPA to ₹11.5 LPA. Premium roles focusing on AI/ML development, enterprise microservices, and distributed computing.

2. Wipro Technologies

Wipro recruits through its Elite National Talent Hunt (NTH) and specialized project drives:

  • Elite Profile: ₹3.5 LPA for Associate Software Engineers.
  • Turbo Profile: ₹6.5 LPA for high performers who excel in advanced algorithmic coding rounds and hackathons.

3. Infosys

Recruits through its campus recruitment process and competitive coding initiatives:

  • Systems Engineer (SE): ₹3.6 LPA.
  • Digital Specialist Engineer (DSE): ₹6.25 LPA.
  • Specialist Programmer (SP): ₹9.5 LPA (Hired through the HackWithInfy nationwide competitive coding tournament).

4. Cognizant, Tech Mahindra & HCLTech

  • Regular recruiters across Bihar GECs offering packages between ₹4.0 LPA and ₹6.5 LPA for roles in application modernization, cloud infrastructure, and full-stack software development.

5. Cisco Systems & Tech Product Firms

  • Visiting premier colleges like MIT Muzaffarpur, Cisco conducts specialized hiring for Network Consulting Engineers and Software Engineers with compensation packages ranging from ₹12 LPA to ₹18 LPA.

The 4-Stage Campus Selection Pipeline

Virtually all major recruiters follow a structured 4-stage screening process:

Round 1: Online Aptitude & Foundational Assessment (Elimination Round)

  • Quantitative Aptitude: Arithmetic, percentages, probability, time-speed-distance, algebra.
  • Logical Reasoning: Syllogisms, blood relations, seating arrangements, coding-decoding.
  • Verbal Ability: Reading comprehension, sentence correction, vocabulary.
  • Basic Coding: 1–2 fundamental programming questions in C++, Java, or Python (Array manipulation, string parsing).

Round 2: Advanced Coding & Technical Assessment

  • For high-paying tiers (TCS Digital/Prime, Infosys SP), candidates must solve 2 to 3 complex algorithmic problems testing Data Structures (Stacks, Queues, Binary Trees, Dynamic Programming, Graphs).

Round 3: Technical Interview

  • Thorough examination of your core coursework: Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), Database Management Systems (DBMS queries & normalization), Operating Systems (Paging, multithreading), Computer Networks.
  • Detailed walkthrough of projects listed on your resume: Architecture, database schema, APIs, and personal contributions.

Round 4: HR & Behavioral Interview

  • Assessment of communication clarity, cultural fit, willingness to relocate, adaptability, and leadership qualities.

12-Month Placement Preparation Action Plan

Follow this structured roadmap starting from your 5th semester:

  • Months 1 to 4 (Semester 5): Master one object-oriented language (C++ or Java). Solve 150+ problems on LeetCode covering Arrays, Strings, HashMaps, Two-Pointers, and Recursion.
  • Months 5 to 7 (Semester 5 Break): Build two production-ready full-stack or domain-specific projects. Host code repositories on GitHub with clear documentation and live deployment links.
  • Months 8 to 10 (Semester 6): Dedicate 45 minutes daily to Quantitative Aptitude practice (IndiaBIX / RS Aggarwal). Study core CS subjects: DBMS (SQL queries), Operating Systems, and OOPs concepts.
  • Months 11 to 12 (Semester 6/7): Take 15+ full-length mock placement assessments. Conduct mock technical interviews with peers and refine your 1-page resume.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Can Civil and Mechanical students participate in IT campus placement drives?

Yes. Most IT mass recruiters (TCS, Wipro, Infosys, Cognizant) permit students from all engineering disciplines to appear for their recruitment tests, provided they meet the minimum CGPA threshold and clear the coding round.

Q2: What is the typical minimum CGPA cutoff required for campus placement eligibility?

Most corporate recruiters enforce a minimum cutoff of 60% or 6.5 CGPA in 10th, 12th, and B.Tech, with no active (standing) backlogs at the time of the recruitment drive. Certain premium roles mandate a 7.0 or 7.5+ CGPA.
