State Government Scholarships for Bihar Engineering Students

📅 Published: 28 June 2026 ✍️ Written by: Er. Prince Kumar (BEU Mentor & ACB Team) ⏱️ 5 min read

Breaking Financial Barriers in Engineering Education

Pursuing a four-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree involves tuition fees, hostel accommodations, mess subscriptions, reference books, and the necessity of purchasing a capable laptop. For thousands of deserving students across Bihar, financial constraints can create significant stress for their families.

Fortunately, the Government of Bihar, under the visionary Seven Resolves (Saat Nischay) initiative, alongside central welfare schemes, provides comprehensive financial aid programs. No student admitted to a government engineering college in Bihar needs to discontinue their education due to financial hardship.

This complete guide details the primary financial aid programs available for BEU students, their eligibility criteria, step-by-step application procedures, and essential document verification checklists.

---

1. Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme (BSCC - MNSSBY)

The Bihar Student Credit Card (BSCC) scheme, administered by the Bihar State Educational Finance Corporation under the Mukhyamantri Nishchay Swayam Sahayata Bhatta Yojana (MNSSBY), is the flagship financial support program for technical students.

Key Features & Benefits:

  • Maximum Loan Amount: Up to ₹4,00,000 (₹4 Lakhs) for the complete 4-year B.Tech course.
  • Highly Subsidized Interest Rates:
  • 1% Simple Interest for female students, transgender applicants, and persons with disabilities (Divyangjan).
  • 4% Simple Interest for male applicants.
  • Government Guarantee: The state government acts as the institutional guarantor. No collateral, property mortgage, or third-party guarantee is required from the student's family.
  • Comprehensive Coverage: Funds cover college tuition fees, examination fees, hostel charges, mess fees, stationery, books, and the purchase of a personal laptop.
  • Moratorium Period: Repayment does not commence immediately. Repayment begins one year after course completion or six months after securing employment, whichever occurs earlier.

Step-by-Step DRCC Application Process:

  1. Online Registration: Visit the official MNSSBY web portal (7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in) and register using your mobile number and Aadhaar card.
  2. Fill Application Details: Select the Bihar Student Credit Card scheme and input personal, academic, college admission, and fee structure details.
  3. Schedule DRCC Appointment: Select an appointment slot to visit your home district's District Registration and Counselling Centre (DRCC).
  4. Physical Verification at DRCC: Report in person to the DRCC with the student applicant and co-applicant (parent/guardian) along with all original documents and self-attested copies.
  5. Loan Approval & Sanction: Following automated verification, a loan agreement is signed, and funds are disbursed in semester installments directly to the college account and student account.

---

2. Bihar Post-Matric Scholarship (PMS)

The Post-Matric Scholarship (PMS) is a direct welfare grant scheme operated by the Education Department of Bihar for students belonging to SC, ST, BC, and EBC categories.

  • Portal: Official Bihar PMS Portal (pmsonline.bih.nic.in).
  • Financial Assistance: Reimburses non-refundable mandatory tuition fees and provides an annual maintenance allowance.
  • Annual Family Income Thresholds:
  • SC / ST Applicants: Total family annual income must not exceed ₹2,50,000.
  • BC / EBC Applicants: Total family annual income must not exceed ₹3,00,000.
  • Key Requirement: Must be applied for every academic year anew.

---

3. AICTE Central Scholarships for Engineering Students

In addition to state-funded schemes, all AICTE-approved engineering colleges in Bihar are eligible for central AICTE welfare scholarships:

A. AICTE Pragati Scholarship Scheme for Girl Students

  • Assistance: ₹50,000 per annum for every year of study (up to 4 years for B.Tech).
  • Eligibility: Female students admitted to 1st year B.Tech or 2nd year (Lateral Entry). Maximum two girls per family. Family income must be below ₹8,00,000 per annum. Selection is based purely on Class 12 qualifying merit.
  • Application: Applied through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP - scholarships.gov.in).

B. AICTE Saksham Scholarship Scheme for Specially-Abled Students

  • Assistance: ₹50,000 per annum.
  • Eligibility: Specially-abled students having a certified disability of not less than 40% with family annual income below ₹8 Lakhs.

---

Mandatory Document Verification Checklist

Assemble these documents before initiating your scholarship or BSCC application:

  1. Aadhaar Card (Applicant and Co-Applicant/Parent).
  2. Permanent Domicile / Residential Certificate of Bihar (Issued at SDO/CO level).
  3. Valid Caste Certificate (Mandatory for SC/ST/BC/EBC scholarship applicants).
  4. Current Financial Year Income Certificate (Issued recently by Revenue Officer/CO).
  5. Class 10th & Class 12th Marksheets and Passing Certificates.
  6. College Admission Slip & Official Bonafide Certificate issued by your college Principal/Registrar.
  7. Approved College Fee Structure Breakdown on official institutional letterhead.
  8. Active Bank Account Passbook belonging exclusively to the student applicant, with Aadhaar NPCI Seeding enabled.
  9. Passport-Sized Photographs (Applicant and Parent).

---

3 Critical Pitfalls That Lead to Application Rejection

  • Failure to Complete Aadhaar NPCI Bank Account Seeding: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scholarship payments fail if your bank account is merely linked with Aadhaar rather than formally seeded with the NPCI mapper. Visit your bank branch and submit the official NPCI seeding form.
  • Expired Income Certificates: Income certificates older than six months or pertaining to a previous financial year are immediately rejected during DRCC or departmental scrutiny.
  • Name & DOB Discrepancies: Ensure the spelling of your name, father's name, and date of birth match character-for-character across your Matriculation certificate, Aadhaar card, and bank account.

---

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Can I avail both the Bihar Student Credit Card loan and the Post-Matric Scholarship simultaneously?

No. State regulations prohibit availing dual state government financial assistance for the same educational tuition fees. If you receive a scholarship that reimburses your tuition, that amount is deducted from the loan disbursement schedule.

Q2: What happens if I am unable to repay the BSCC loan after completing B.Tech?

The scheme includes an institutional grievance redressal mechanism. If a graduate remains unemployed despite diligent efforts, they can submit an affidavit to the DRCC for an extension of the moratorium period.
