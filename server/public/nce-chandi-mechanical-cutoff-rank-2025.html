🏢 Branch Internal Overview & Academics

B.Tech Mechanical Engineering at NCE Chandi offers robust mechanical system fundamentals, covering Thermodynamics, Machine Design, Fluid Mechanics, Kinematics, Heat Transfer, and CAD/CAM modeling structures.

👨‍🏫 Faculty & Mentorship Profile

The Mechanical department is staffed by seasoned academic instructors with research interests in thermal sciences, material sciences, and automation. Professors provide supportive mentoring for engineering design projects and technical events.

🔬 Laboratory & Practical Infrastructure

Lab facilities include a fully functional Workshop (Smithy, Carpentry, Fitting), Internal Combustion Engine labs, Machine Kinematics tools, Heat Transfer rigs, and CAD workstations equipped with standard industry drafting software.

💼 Branch Placement Statistics

Mechanical graduates consistently secure job offers in core industrial houses (like Tata Motors, L&T, Prism Cement), energy companies, or slide into software roles during central pool placement drives.
