UGEAC 2026 Result Kaise Check Karein — Step by Step

  1. Official Website par jaayen: BCECEB ki official website (bceceboard.bihar.gov.in) ya UGEAC counselling portal open karein.
  2. Login karein: Apna UGEAC registration number aur password se login karein.
  3. Seat Allotment Result dekhein: Dashboard par allotted college name, branch, aur seat type (Government/Private) dikhega.
  4. Allotment Letter Download karein: PDF format mein allotment letter download karein — college reporting ke liye zaroori hai.
  5. Action Choose karein: Accept (seat confirm), Float (next round ke liye wait), ya Withdraw (seat chhodna) choose karein.
⚠️ Important Warning!

Seat accept karne ke baad online fee payment deadline miss mat karein — warna seat cancel ho jaayegi! Fee payment ke baad college mein original documents ke saath reporting zaroori hai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: UGEAC 2026 seat allotment result kab aayega?
UGEAC 2026 Round 1 result July–August 2026 mein expected hai. JEE Mains result aur UGEAC registration ke baad dates announce hoti hain. Apna College Bihar par updates milenge.
Q: UGEAC mein Float option choose karna chahiye ya Freeze?
Float recommend kiya jaata hai! Float karke aap current seat keep karte hain aur next round mein better college ke liye eligible rehte hain. Koi risk nahi — worst case wahi college milega jo pehle mila tha.
Q: UGEAC 2026 mein kitne rounds hote hain?
UGEAC mein typically 3–5 rounds hote hain. Har round ke baad vacant seats fill hoti hain. Last mein Spot Round bhi hota hai jisme leftover seats college level par allot hoti hain.
