AICTE 160-Credit Category Breakdown for B.Tech Degree

Under the academic regulations of Bihar Engineering University (BEU Patna), a student must successfully earn a minimum of 160 academic credits across 8 semesters to be awarded the degree of Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech). The curriculum adheres strictly to the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) model structure:

Course Category Code Category Classification Curriculum Focus & Representative Subjects Required Credits
BSC Basic Science Courses Engineering Mathematics (I, II, III), Engineering Physics, Engineering Chemistry 25 Credits
ESC Engineering Science Courses Basic Electrical Engineering, Programming for Problem Solving (PPS), Workshop, Engineering Graphics 24 Credits
HSMC Humanities & Social Sciences English for Technical Communication, Economics for Engineers, Constitution of India, Universal Human Values 12 Credits
PCC Professional Core Courses Core discipline fundamental subjects (e.g. Data Structures, Operating Systems, Fluid Mechanics, Thermodynamics) 48 Credits
PEC Professional Elective Courses Advanced departmental specializations (e.g. Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, Earthquake Engineering, Robotics) 18 Credits
OEC Open Elective Courses Interdisciplinary courses offered across departments (e.g. IoT, Project Management, Renewable Energy, Soft Computing) 18 Credits
PROJ / INT Project, Seminar & Internship Summer Industrial Internships (4 to 8 weeks), Technical Seminars, Capstone Major Project (Part I & II) 15 Credits

Official BEU 10-Point Absolute Grading System

Academic performance in each course is evaluated through continuous internal assessment (30 marks) and external end-semester theory examinations (70 marks). Letter grades and grade points are awarded based on absolute score percentages:

Academic Letter Grade Grade Point (Gi) Percentage Score Bracket Qualitative Performance Description
O (Outstanding) 10 90% to 100% Extraordinary conceptual mastery and flawless answer presentation
A+ (Excellent) 9 80% to 89% Thorough conceptual grasp and high analytical execution
A (Very Good) 8 70% to 79% Strong academic knowledge and accurate problem solving
B+ (Good) 7 60% to 69% Above-average competency across core syllabus modules
B (Above Average) 6 50% to 59% Satisfactory performance meeting basic course objectives
C (Average) 5 45% to 49% Fair comprehension with moderate errors in analytical derivations
P (Pass) 4 40% to 44% Minimum passing benchmark satisfying university evaluation threshold
F (Fail / Backlog) 0 Below 40% (or <25/70 in External) Unsuccessful; candidate must clear subject in carry-over exams

Official Promotion & Year-Back Regulations

Bihar Engineering University follows explicit credit accumulation rules for semester promotion:

  • A student is promoted from 1st Year (Semester 2) to 2nd Year (Semester 3) only if they secure at least 50% of total 1st-year credits (typically 18+ credits out of 35).
  • Promotion from 2nd Year to 3rd Year requires clearing all 1st-year backlog courses with zero active backlogs in 1st year subjects.
  • A maximum duration of 7 academic years is permitted to complete the 4-year B.Tech program.

Bihar Engineering University (BEU) Examination & Grading Architecture

Understanding the university grading scheme is vital to achieving a top academic record. BEU operates under the standardized Choice Based Credit System (CBCS):

  • 70/30 Examination Distribution: Every theory subject carries 100 marks, divided into 70 marks for the centralized End-Semester University Examination and 30 marks for Internal Continuous Assessment (Attendance, Mid-Sem, and Lab Viva).
  • Passing Thresholds: A student must secure a minimum of 35% marks (25 out of 70) in the external theory exam AND an aggregate of 40% overall (Internal + External combined) to pass the subject.
  • The 75% Attendance Ordinance: BEU Academic Ordinance Regulation 6.1 strictly mandates a minimum of 75% class attendance. Shortfall up to 10% may be condoned by the college Principal solely on certified medical grounds.
  • Grade Points Scale: University results are awarded on a 10-point scale: 'O' (Outstanding, 10 GP), 'A+' (Excellent, 9 GP), 'A' (Very Good, 8 GP), 'B+' (Good, 7 GP), 'B' (Above Average, 6 GP), 'C' (Average, 5 GP), 'P' (Pass, 4 GP), and 'F' (Fail, 0 GP).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: How can I score above a 9.0 CGPA in BEU Semester Examinations?

To achieve a 9+ CGPA, focus on three pillars: First, systematically practice 5 years of Previous Year Questions (PYQs) from the ACB portal, as 60% of core concepts repeat. Second, illustrate all theoretical answers with neat pencil diagrams, block schematics, and boxed numerical solutions. Third, score at least 26/30 in internal assessments by maintaining 75%+ attendance and submitting practical records on time.

Q2: What is the official passing marks criteria in BEU examinations?

Under BEU regulations, you must score at least 25 out of 70 marks in the external theory exam AND achieve at least 40 marks combined with internal continuous evaluation to pass a subject. Scoring below 25 in externals results in an 'F' grade (backlog) regardless of internal marks.

Q3: Can I get admission to Bihar government engineering colleges without JEE Main?

No. Under current BCECEB regulations, admission to government B.Tech programs in Bihar requires a valid NTA JEE Main scorecard. BCECEB conducts centralized UGEAC counselling strictly based on JEE Main ranks.

Q4: How do I convert BEU CGPA into percentage?

According to standard AICTE and university guidelines, the standard conversion formula is: Percentage = (CGPA - 0.75) × 10. For certain recruitment drives (such as TCS, Wipro, Infosys), direct multiplication by 9.5 or 10 may be accepted. Use our official BEU CGPA Calculator to verify your scores.

Q5: What are the top government engineering colleges under BEU?

The premier government engineering colleges in Bihar include Muzaffarpur Institute of Technology (MIT Muzaffarpur, Estd. 1954), Bhagalpur College of Engineering (BCE Bhagalpur, Estd. 1960), Gaya College of Engineering (GCE Gaya), Darbhanga College of Engineering (DCE Darbhanga), and Bakhtiyarpur College of Engineering (BCE Patna).
