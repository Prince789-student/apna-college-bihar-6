About the Creator & Academic Mission

Er. Prince Kumar founded Apna College Bihar (ACB) with a singular, transformative vision: to bridge the resource gap for thousands of undergraduate engineers studying across Bihar's 38 Government Engineering Colleges (GECs) affiliated with Bihar Engineering University (BEU Patna).

Having experienced the unique hurdles of state university engineering education firsthand — fragmented syllabus updates, scarce semester question papers, confusing counselling cutoffs, and the lack of organized semester notes — Prince spearheaded the creation of Bihar's most comprehensive digital academic hub. Today, Apna College Bihar serves more than 20,000+ engineering scholars across all branches, offering 100% free lecture notes, previous year question papers (PYQs), CGPA calculators, and real UGEAC counselling prediction tools.

Editorial Standards & Fact-Checking Process

At Apna College Bihar, every study material, syllabus breakdown, cutoff analysis, and exam strategy published under Prince Kumar's guidance adheres to rigorous academic standards:

  • ✓ University Curriculum Alignment: All subject modules, unit divisions, and topic outlines are audited strictly against official BEU AICTE course structure documents.
  • ✓ Exam PYQ Integrity: Question papers from previous university semesters are collected from genuine examination centres across Bihar, digitized via optical character recognition (OCR), cross-checked for missing pages, and verified against university question code patterns.
  • ✓ Cutoff & Counselling Accuracy: UGEAC opening and closing ranks are compiled directly from BCECEB allotment matrices across multiple rounds (General, EWS, BC, EBC, SC, ST) with no speculative or fabricated estimations.
  • ✓ Transparent Peer Mentorship: A dedicated network of top-ranking senior scholars from MIT Muzaffarpur, BCE Bhagalpur, and BCE Bakhtiyarpur assists in review passes before educational content is uploaded.
