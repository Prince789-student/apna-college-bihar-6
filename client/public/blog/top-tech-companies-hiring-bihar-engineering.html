Top Tech Companies Hiring in Bihar Engineering Colleges

📅 Published: 28 June 2026 ✍️ Written by: Er. Prince Kumar (BEU Mentor & ACB Team) ⏱️ 5 min read

The Placement Scenario in Bihar

The placement ecosystem in Bihar's government engineering colleges (like MIT Muzaffarpur, BCE Bhagalpur, and GCE Gaya) has seen significant improvement. With active Training and Placement (T&P) cells, many top-tier companies are now participating in campus drives.

Top IT Recruiters

For Computer Science (CSE) and Information Technology (IT) students, the service-based IT sector is the largest recruiter. However, students from core branches with good coding skills are also eligible.

  • TCS (Tata Consultancy Services): One of the mass recruiters. They usually hire through the TCS NQT (National Qualifier Test) for Ninja and Digital roles.
  • Wipro & Infosys: Regular visitors offering standard associate software engineer roles.
  • Cognizant & Tech Mahindra: Frequently recruit for both software development and testing profiles.
  • Cisco: Often recruits from top tier colleges like MIT Muzaffarpur for high-paying network and software roles.

Top Core Companies

For Civil, Mechanical, and Electrical students, core placements are highly competitive but rewarding.

  • L&T (Larsen & Toubro): Hires Civil and Mechanical engineers for major infrastructure projects.
  • Alstom: Recruits Electrical and Mechanical engineers.
  • Prism Johnson & ACC Cements: Frequent recruiters for Civil engineering graduates.

How to Prepare?

  1. Maintain a Minimum 7.0 CGPA: Most companies have a strict cutoff of 65% or 7.0 CGPA with no active backlogs.
  2. Master Aptitude & Reasoning: The first round for almost every company is a quantitative aptitude test.
  3. Build Projects: For IT roles, having 2-3 solid projects on your resume (e.g., a Web App or Machine Learning model) will make you stand out during technical interviews.

Stay active on LinkedIn and keep an eye on your college's T&P notice board!
