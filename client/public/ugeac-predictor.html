Understanding the BCECEB UGEAC Counselling Lifecycle

The Undergraduate Engineering Admission Counselling (UGEAC) is conducted annually by the Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB). Admission into all 38 Government Engineering Colleges (GECs) under Bihar Engineering University is determined exclusively by merit in the National Testing Agency's (NTA) JEE Main exam.

Mandatory Document Verification Checklist

Keep 3 sets of self-attested photocopies ready for Nodal Center physical reporting:

  • NTA JEE Main Admit Card & Official Scorecard
  • BCECEB UGEAC Rank Card & Seat Allotment Letter
  • Class 10th Marksheet & Passing Certificate (DOB proof)
  • Class 12th (10+2) Marksheet & Passing Certificate
  • Permanent Residential / Domicile Certificate of Bihar (CO/SDO/DM level)
  • Caste / Non-Creamy Layer Certificate (for BC, EBC, SC, ST) or EWS Asset Certificate
  • College/School Leaving Certificate (CLC/TC) & Character Certificate
  • 6 Passport Size Photographs matching JEE Main admit card
