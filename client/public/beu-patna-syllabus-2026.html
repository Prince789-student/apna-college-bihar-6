BEU New Syllabus 2026 — Kya Naya Hai?

  • Outcome Based Education (OBE): Course Outcomes (CO) aur Program Outcomes (PO) mapping
  • Industry Relevant Subjects: AI, ML, Cloud Computing, IoT, Cyber Security jaise subjects add huye
  • Practical Weightage Increase: Lab aur practical ka weightage 40% tak badha
  • Choice Based Credit System (CBCS): Students ko elective subjects choose karne ka option
  • Mini Project & Internship: 6th semester se mandatory mini project aur industrial internship

Q: BEU new syllabus kab se lagu hua?
BEU ka naya syllabus 2022 session se implement hua hai. Yeh AICTE aur NEP 2020 guidelines ke according hai. 2026 mein bhi yahi syllabus follow hota hai with minor updates.
Q: BEU old syllabus aur new syllabus mein kya difference hai?
