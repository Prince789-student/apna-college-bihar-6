About Muzaffarpur Institute of Technology

Muzaffarpur Institute of Technology (MIT Muzaffarpur), established in 1954, is one of the oldest and most prestigious government engineering colleges in Eastern India. Situated across a sprawling 65-acre campus in Brahmpura, Muzaffarpur, it offers premier B.Tech, M.Tech, and B.Pharm programs under Bihar Engineering University.

Admission & Counselling Process

Admissions to B.Tech courses at MIT Muzaffarpur are conducted centrally through the BCECEB UGEAC (Under Graduate Engineering Admission Counselling) based on JEE Main scores. Eligible candidates must register for counselling, complete document verification, and fill choice preferences.
