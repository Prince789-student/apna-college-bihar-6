Bihar Engineering University (BEU) B.Tech Syllabus 2026-2026

Bihar ke sabhi government aur private engineering colleges ab Bihar Engineering University (BEU), Patna (pehle AKU Patna) ke antargat aate hain. BEU ka B.Tech syllabus modern AICTE model curriculum par aadharit hai jisme students ko theoretical knowledge ke saath-saath practical aur industrial skills par focus kiya jata hai.

BEU 1st Year Syllabus (Group A vs Group B)

BEU me 1st year (Semester 1 aur Semester 2) me students ko do groups me banta jata hai taaki laboratories aur classes smoothly chal sakein:

  • Group A (Electrical, Mechanical, CSE, EEE, ECE): 1st semester me Chemistry, Mathematics-I (Calculus & Linear Algebra), Programming for Problem Solving (PPS), aur English padhaya jata hai.
  • Group B (Civil & Allied Branches): 1st semester me Semiconductor Physics, Mathematics-I (Calculus & Differential Equations), Basic Electrical Engineering, aur Engineering Graphics & Design padhaya jata hai.

Branch Wise Core Syllabus (3rd Sem to 8th Sem)

💻 Computer Science Engineering (CSE)

3rd semester me Data Structures, OOPs (C++/Java), Analog & Digital Electronics, aur Mathematics-III. 4th sem me Discrete Mathematics, Computer Organization (COA), Operating Systems, aur Design & Analysis of Algorithms (DAA).

🏗️ Civil Engineering

3rd sem me Basic Electronics, Engineering Mechanics, Surveying & Geomatics, aur Building Materials. 4th sem me Fluid Mechanics, Solid Mechanics, Disaster Management, aur Environmental Engineering.

⚙️ Mechanical Engineering

3rd sem me Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Materials Engineering, aur Machine Drawing. 4th sem me Applied Thermodynamics, Strength of Materials (SOM), Kinematics of Machines, aur Instrumentation & Control.

⚡ Electrical Engineering (EE & EEE)

3rd sem me Electric Circuit Analysis, Analog Electronics, Electrical Machines-I, aur Electromagnetic Fields. 4th sem me Digital Electronics, Electrical Machines-II, Power Electronics, aur Signals & Systems.
