GCE Gaya ke Baare Mein — Overview

Government College of Engineering Gaya (GCE Gaya) Bihar ke premier state engineering colleges mein se ek hai. Yeh Bihar Engineering University (BEU) se affiliated hai aur UGEAC counselling ke through admission hota hai.

  • Location: Gaya, Bihar — ek historically aur educationally important city
  • Established: Government funded, state-level engineering college
  • Affiliation: Bihar Engineering University (BEU), Patna
  • Branches: CSE, ECE, EE, ME, CE (5 major branches)
  • Admission: Only through UGEAC 2025 counselling (JEE Mains based)
  • Placement: Active placement cell with core + IT companies
