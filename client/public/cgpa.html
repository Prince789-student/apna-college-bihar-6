Official University SGPA & CGPA Calculation Formulas

Bihar Engineering University uses a credit-weighted grade point average system. Your semester standing (SGPA) and degree standing (CGPA) are computed strictly using the following mathematical formulations:

Semester Formula

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average)

SGPA = Σ(Ci × Gi) / Σ(Ci)

Where Ci is the credit of course i and Gi is the grade point earned in that course.

Cumulative Formula

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average)

CGPA = Σ(Sj × Cj) / Σ(Cj)

Where Sj is the SGPA of semester j and Cj is the total credits registered in semester j.

Official BEU CGPA to Percentage Conversion

Under official Bihar Engineering University and AICTE notifications, to convert your Cumulative Grade Point Average into an equivalent marks percentage:

Equivalent Percentage (%) = (CGPA - 0.75) × 10

Example: If your CGPA is 8.50 → Percentage = (8.50 - 0.75) × 10 = 7.75 × 10 = 77.50%
